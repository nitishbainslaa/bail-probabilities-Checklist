AI Bail Probability Checker is an interactive legal-assistance tool designed to provide users with an initial assessment of their potential bail prospects. The platform allows users to enter key case details such as case type, previous criminal record, arrest status, FIR registration, charge-sheet status, and a brief case summary. Based on these inputs, the tool generates a Bail Probability Score along with an assessment of case strength and legal risk. The result is categorized as High, Moderate, or Low Bail Probability, helping users understand the general position of their case at an initial level. The platform also highlights positive and risk factors that may influence bail proceedings, including criminal history, arrest circumstances, investigation status, possible court objections, documentation, and legal preparation. In addition, users can access information relating to section-wise bail analysis, relevant Supreme Court judgments, Sessions Court strategy, and legal drafting guidance. The complete advocate report can also be requested through WhatsApp for further assistance.
